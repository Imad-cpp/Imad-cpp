## Context

**Nexar** is a gaming-hall project I founded in Morocco with the goal of creating a modern, well-structured gaming experience.

**Role:** Founder

## <img src="../../assets/icons/product-engineering.svg" width="20" alt=""> Why it belongs in my engineering profile

Nexar is different from N9raw and Nour FPN. It is **not presented here as a software platform case study**.

It belongs in my profile because it reflects the **product and founder side** of how I work: turning an idea into a defined customer experience, thinking about operations, positioning and how technology supports a physical business.

## <img src="../../assets/icons/product-engineering.svg" width="20" alt=""> Product problem

A gaming hall is not only a room with gaming PCs or consoles. The quality of the experience depends on many connected decisions:

- equipment and performance;
- network reliability;
- layout and comfort;
- session/customer flow;
- identity and atmosphere;
- operations and maintenance; and
- the way the venue is positioned for its local audience.

Nexar is my exploration of how these parts can be designed as one experience rather than assembled independently.

## <img src="../../assets/icons/product-engineering.svg" width="20" alt=""> My role

As founder, my work around Nexar includes thinking through:

- the concept and positioning;
- the target customer experience;
- operational requirements;
- technology and infrastructure needs;
- brand/digital presence; and
- how the project can become a repeatable, sustainable business rather than only a one-time setup.

## <img src="../../assets/icons/infrastructure.svg" width="20" alt=""> Operations and technology context

The project connects a physical customer experience with practical technology and infrastructure considerations.

That includes thinking about equipment performance, network reliability, maintenance, venue operations and the digital presence that supports the business — without treating those considerations as proof of a completed software platform.

## <img src="../../assets/icons/cybersecurity.svg" width="20" alt=""> Engineering evidence boundary

I intentionally do **not** list a software architecture or technology stack for Nexar here because the current verified project evidence does not justify presenting it as a completed software system.

When a technical system is actually designed and implemented for Nexar, this case study can be updated with evidence-backed architecture and implementation detail.

That boundary is part of the portfolio standard: **founder/product evidence should not be turned into software claims only to make a project look more technical**.

## <img src="../../assets/icons/verified-learning.svg" width="20" alt=""> What this project demonstrates

Nexar adds a different dimension to my profile:

- founder thinking;
- physical + digital product experience;
- operational planning;
- customer-experience design;
- technology decisions in a real-world venue context; and
- discipline about separating an idea from what is actually implemented.

## Current state

Nexar is an active founder project. Public claims in this case study are intentionally limited to the project direction and my role until more implementation evidence is ready to publish.
